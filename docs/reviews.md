# Personal ratings and reviews

Each game has one personal review in this browser, stored under `backlog:review:<game-id>`. Ratings are whole stars from 0 to 5; null means no rating. A rating or written text is required, and text is limited to 3,000 characters. Saving, editing and deleting reviews never changes collection statuses. Reviews are private browser data and are not published or synced to an account.

The game page includes a Your review tab, keyboard-accessible native rating inputs (0–5), a live text counter, a saved review preview, edit/cancel controls and inline delete confirmation. The summary shows your personal rating rather than a community average. Written content is rendered as text. Storage failures leave the editor contents intact.

Validated across all 40 games, with responsive layouts at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels. Checked zero/rating-only/text-only reviews, save and reload, editing and cancellation, confirmed deletion, per-game isolation, existing collection statuses, cross-tab updates, malformed storage and storage failures.
