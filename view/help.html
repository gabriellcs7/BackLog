HELP & FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Guides and answers for managing your catalog, diary entries, and account.

Take a tour ↗Open settings ↗

Do I need an account?+

You can keep a device profile without signing in. An online account syncs your profile and enables follows, likes and comments.

How do game statuses work?+

Choose Played, Playing, Completed, Platinum, Dropped or Want to play on a game page. You can change the status later.

How do I write a review?+

Open a game and select Write a review. You can give half-star ratings, choose the review date and mark spoilers before saving.

What does Discover do?+

Discover shows games outside your collection. Swipe right to add a game to Want to play, left to skip, or use the buttons and Undo. Skipping does not hide a game permanently.

Can I create a ranked list?+

Create a list from your profile and enable ranking. Add games, arrange their order and save the list.

Where are my statistics?+

Open View statistics in Your gaming journal on a profile. Choose a year to explore its logged games, ratings, genres and recorded completions.

Why are the news not updating?+

Automatic news needs an internet connection and the BackLog local server. Start python servidor.py from the project folder and reload the site.

How do I export my data?+

Open Settings and choose Export my data. The backup includes the selected profile data, lists, games, reviews and diary available to that account or device.

Looking for project instructions?

The repository has the local setup guide and feature documentation.
